肌纤维损伤区定量 v2.3.0Muscle fiber injury quantification

按WGA轮廓分割每根肌纤维，以中心区有无细胞核界定损伤纤维，计算红色阳性面积占损伤区面积的比例。图像只在本机浏览器中处理，不会上传。Segments fibers from WGA outlines, defines injured fibers by nuclei in the fiber core, and measures red-positive area as a share of the injured area. Images are processed locally in your browser and never uploaded.

3分割与判定参数Segmentation and classification

纤维分割Fiber segmentation
可封住WGA环上宽度约为2倍此值的缺口；纤维随后自动长回原轮廓。建议约4 µm对应的像素数（0.9 µm/像素时取4–5）。半径小于此值的小纤维会丢失。Closes gaps in WGA rings up to about twice this width; fibers then grow back to their outlines. About 4 µm in pixels is a good start (4–5 at 0.9 µm/px). Fibers with a radius below this value are lost.
下限按膜内侧面积计算，用于排除纤维之间的小缝隙；分析很小的再生纤维时调低。The minimum applies to the area inside the membrane and removes small gaps between fibers; lower it for very small regenerating fibers.
大的膜加粗会把小纤维整个腐蚀掉。第二遍只在第一遍没有纤维的地方补充，补出的区域同样经过面积、形状和细胞核筛选。Large dilation erases small fibers. The second pass only adds regions where the first found no fiber, and they pass the same area, shape and nuclear filters.
纤维与间质分离Fiber–interstitium separation
WGA环不完整时，纤维会经缺口与间质连成一片。先把"膜加粗"调到约4 µm对应的像素数来封住缺口，再用下面两项剔除被误当成纤维的间质。When WGA rings are incomplete, fibers merge with the interstitium through the gaps. First set membrane dilation to about 4 µm in pixels to close the gaps, then use these two filters to reject interstitium mistaken for fibers.
凸度 = 面积 ÷ 凸包面积，圆形、椭圆形纤维约为0.94–0.98；被几根纤维夹住的缝隙边缘内凹，通常低于0.9。设为0则不筛选。Solidity = area ÷ convex-hull area: about 0.94–0.98 for round or oval fibers, usually below 0.9 for gaps squeezed between fibers. 0 disables.
区域内能放下的最大圆的半径。相邻纤维之间的缝隙很窄，低于此值的区域不算纤维；设为0则不筛选。Radius of the largest circle that fits in the region. Gaps between neighboring fibers are narrow; regions below this value are rejected. 0 disables.
形状指数 = 面积 ÷ 最大内切圆面积，圆形为1，不规则的间质网络很大。超过此值的区域不算纤维；设为0则不筛选。Area ÷ area of the largest inscribed circle: 1 for a circle, large for branching interstitium. Regions above this value are rejected; 0 disables.
细胞核占比超过此值的区域视为间质；设为0则不筛选。Regions with more nuclear area are treated as interstitium; 0 disables.
中央核判定Central nuclei
从纤维边缘向内缩，排除膜下的正常边缘核。小纤维的内缩距离自动封顶为其内切半径的40%。Shrinks each outline inward to exclude normal subsarcolemmal nuclei. For small fibers the inset is capped at 40% of the inscribed radius.
阳性标记Positive marker
判定阳性时把纤维轮廓向外扩这么多，用于红色集中在纤维外缘、落在分割轮廓之外的情况。建议1–3 µm；只影响阳性判定，不改变纤维面积。Extends each outline outward when calling red+, for red signal at the fiber rim just outside the segmented outline. 1–3 µm is typical; it affects only the call, not fiber area.
比上面的红色阈值更严格，默认在红色阳性像素中再做一次Otsu，避免小纤维与周围淡红色背景连成一片。漏检时调低，误检时调高。Stricter than the red threshold above: by default a second Otsu among red+ pixels, so small fibers do not merge with faint red background. Lower it if fibers are missed, raise it if false objects appear.
不与已识别纤维相连、大致呈圆形（内切半径≥2.5 µm、形状指数≤3）、且不完全落在WGA膜上的红色阳性小块（包括中间空心的环形）会被补充为纤维。Red+ objects, including rings with an unstained center, are added as fibers if they do not touch a detected fiber, are roughly round (inscribed radius ≥ 2.5 µm, shape index ≤ 3) and do not lie mostly on the WGA membrane.
按像素：只计红色阳性像素。阳性纤维整根：判为阳性的纤维按整根面积计入。两种方式下，手动标为阳性的纤维都按整根面积计入，手动标为阴性的不计入。Pixels counts red+ pixels only; whole red+ fibers counts the full area of each red+ fiber. In both, fibers marked red+ by hand count in full and fibers marked red− count as zero.

方法说明与注意事项Method and notes

纤维分割：WGA通道扣除背景并平滑后，在强度图上寻找被亮环包围的暗区作为每根纤维的中心（h-minima），再从这些中心做分水岭，纤维边界落在WGA膜的中线；以0.7倍Otsu阈值得到膜掩膜，用于计算膜内侧面积、到膜的距离等。最后按膜内侧面积、内切半径、凸度和形状剔除纤维间的缝隙与间质。中央核判定：距膜超过设定距离的中心区内，细胞核面积达到设定值即判为中央核纤维。损伤区为全部中央核纤维的并集。

Fiber segmentation: after background subtraction and smoothing of the WGA channel, dark regions surrounded by brighter rings are taken as fiber centers (h-minima), and a watershed from these centers places each fiber boundary on the middle of the WGA membrane. A membrane mask at 0.7 × the Otsu threshold gives the area inside the membrane and the distance to the membrane. Gaps between fibers and interstitium are rejected by inner area, inscribed radius, solidity and shape. Central nuclei: a fiber is centrally nucleated when the set nuclear area lies in its core, farther than the set distance from the membrane. The injured region is the union of all centrally nucleated fibers.

红色阳性面积只统计纤维内部的像素，纤维间隙中的红色信号不计入。自动阈值使用Otsu法；红色信号稀疏时自动阈值可能偏低，请打开"红色阳性像素"叠加层核对后手动调整。

Red-positive area counts only pixels inside fibers; red signal in the interstitium is ignored. Automatic thresholds use Otsu's method, which can run low when red signal is sparse; check the "Red+ pixels" overlay and adjust manually.

同一批实验请使用相同的参数，并先用几张图人工核对自动判定结果。统计时以动物为单位，不要以切片或视野为单位。

无纤维损伤区：手动圈画的区域和自动识别的DAPI密集区中，不属于任何已识别纤维的像素计入损伤区面积，其中的红色像素计入红色阳性面积。汇总中会单独列出这部分面积。

Fiber-free injured areas: pixels in drawn regions or auto-detected DAPI-dense areas that do not belong to any detected fiber are added to the injured area, and their red pixels to the red+ area. The summary lists this part separately.

手动圈画：漏识别的纤维可用"圈画纤维"沿纤维内缘圈出，圈内尚未属于其他纤维的像素组成一根新纤维，并按同样规则判定中央核和红色阳性；漏掉的红色信号可用"圈画红色阳性"圈出，圈内像素计为红色阳性。所有圈画都记录在CSV中，"撤销"按圈画顺序逐个删除。

Manual drawing: trace a missed fiber along its inner edge with "Draw fiber"; pixels inside that do not belong to another fiber form a new fiber, classified by the same rules. Outline missed red signal with "Draw red+"; pixels inside count as red+. All drawings are recorded in the CSV, and Undo removes them in reverse order.

Cellpose分割：通过本机运行的 cellpose_server.py 打开本页时，可直接点"用Cellpose分割（本机GPU）"；也可导入Cellpose输出的标签图代替内置分割。导入后，纤维边界以Cellpose为准，但每个对象只保留其内部和靠近自身一侧的一半WGA膜，Cellpose划入的纤维间隙会被去除；之后只排除接触图像边缘、过小或过大的对象；中央核、红色阳性、损伤区和面积测量照常进行。

Cellpose segmentation: when this page is opened through cellpose_server.py on your computer, "Segment with Cellpose (local GPU)" runs Cellpose directly; alternatively a Cellpose label image can be imported in place of the built-in segmentation. Fiber outlines then come from Cellpose, but each object keeps only its interior and the half of the WGA membrane on its own side, so gaps that Cellpose assigned to fibers are removed; only objects touching the image edge or outside the area range are excluded; central nuclei, red positivity, the injured region and area measurements are computed as usual.

Use the same parameters across an experiment and verify the automatic calls on a few images first. Treat the animal, not the section or field, as the statistical unit.
